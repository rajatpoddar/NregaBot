# Brag Plan: NREGA Bot

## What is this app?
NREGA Bot is a powerful desktop automation tool for Windows, macOS, and Linux that eliminates manual data entry on the Indian MGNREGA / VB-G-RAM-G portal for Gram Rozgar Sevaks and Panchayats.

## The angle
High-energy desktop software product film: "What takes hours of manual portal entry finishes in seconds — 48+ tasks automated in true background mode."

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Big bold typography on dark slate: "Hours of MGNREGA portal entry?" → STAMP: "Done in seconds."

## Key moments (the middle)
- **48+ Portal Automations**: Live tab switcher grid (Muster Roll, Demand, eMB Entry, Zero MR, FTO Gen).
- **True Background Mode & Retry**: Browser stays minimized, 1-click Retry Failed, and instant WhatsApp Excel reports.
- **Smart Updates & Security**: SHA-256 verified KB-size hotfix updates + PII masking.

## Outro / punchline
"Stop typing. Start automating." → NREGA Bot v3.2.9 Logo & Download CTA.

## User flow worth showing
Select Panchayat & Work Code → Click Start Automation → Browser automates background tasks → Instant print-ready Excel & PDF reports delivered.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: "high-energy desktop software product film"
- Interpretation: Clean typography, fast-in cards, bold blue accents, smooth motion with high legibility.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#0F172A`
- Accent: `#3B82F6`
- Text: `#F8FAFC`
- Display font: `Inter`
- Body font: `Inter`
- Strongest visual element: Modern dark-mode dashboard cards with live status indicators and badge pills.

## Share copy (draft)
Say goodbye to manual MGNREGA portal entry. NREGA Bot v3.2.9 automates 48+ tasks in seconds in true background mode. 🚜⚡

## Audio direction
- Role: Warm energetic bed with crisp UI click & success sound accents.
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: Fade in 0.5s, upbeat steady volume, fade out at 20s.
- Music cue guidance: Upbeat track at ~120 BPM; target cues at 3s (hook stamp), 8s (feature grid), 15s (results pop), 18s (logo drop).
- Audio-reactive treatment: Subtle pulse on active status pills.
- SFX posture: Moderate, motion-matched click & swell sounds.
- Audio-coupled moments: Button clicks, card reveals, status checkmarks.
- Restraint rule: Audio stays supportive, never overwhelming.

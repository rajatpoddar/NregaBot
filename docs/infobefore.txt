                    WELCOME TO NREGA BOT

Thank you for choosing NREGA Bot -- your desktop automation
assistant for the MGNREGA / VB-G-RAM-G portal.

This wizard will install NREGA Bot on your computer.

The application will be installed in the following location:
    [Program Files]\NREGA Bot

A shortcut will be added to your Start Menu, and you will have the
option to create a desktop icon.

------------------------------------------------------------------

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
    - Windows 10 or later (64-bit)
    - 4 GB RAM (8 GB recommended)
    - Google Chrome installed
    - Active internet connection

A Lite version of the app is also available for low-end devices
with limited memory.
